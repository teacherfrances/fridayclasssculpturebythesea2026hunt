Welcome back

How the hunt works
  1. Stay with your classmates. Stay on the path and never walk alone.
  2. On the way there, practise with any classmate. At the exhibition, ask strangers: visitors, volunteers and staff. Ask even if you already know the answer. That is how you practise!
  3. Do the tasks in any order. You do not need to finish all 40.
  4. Use the SAY line. Change the words to suit you.
  5. The highlighted words are expressions from Lesson 1, The Last Lap. Try to use all 20 today.
  6. Write a short answer. Notes are fine.
  7. Add a photo as proof. Take photos of sculptures, signs and views. You can take photos of yourself and your classmates, but ask them first. Do not take photos of strangers.
  8. For a Talk task, take a photo of the sculpture or sign you talked about.
  9. Look, but do not touch or climb on the sculptures.
  10. Watch the time. Be at the meeting place on time.
  11. Your answers and photos save on this phone. You can close the page and come back.
